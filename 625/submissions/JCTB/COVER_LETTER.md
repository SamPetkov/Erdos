Dear Editors,

Please consider my manuscript, “A Full-Sequence Quantitative Gap Between the Chromatic and Cochromatic Numbers of a Random Graph,” for publication in the *Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B*.

The paper addresses Erdős Problem 625, originating in a question of Erdős and Gimbel about the chromatic and cochromatic numbers of the binomial random graph G(n,1/2). It establishes that, along the full sequence of integers n, the difference is at least 0.063 n/(log n)^3 with high probability, matching the order conjectured by Heckel. The manuscript also gives a phase-resolved lower bound and summable exceptional probabilities, which imply an almost-sure eventual lower bound under any coupling.

The argument uses an optimization over five consecutive class sizes to obtain a uniform gain across rounding phases. A signed second-moment analysis controls common whole classes, large cells in overlap matrices, and residual overlaps. It then amplifies the resulting lower probability of a marked cocoloring into a high-probability estimate.

A companion Lean 4 development verifies a weaker explicit full-sequence quantitative consequence. The manuscript identifies the stronger refinements that are not covered by the formalized theorem, and includes a disclosure of computational and AI-assisted work.

The work is available as a preprint at https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30604, with source and reproducibility material at https://github.com/SamPetkov/Erdos/tree/main/625.

The arrows denote theorem dependency, not equality. Each symbol has a single
responsibility. In particular, the endpoint and residual charges are not both
assigned to the same overlap feature.

The root transport now consumes the finite-dual output of Section~3 directly,
and the global second-moment ledger consumes the three-range output of
Section~7 directly. This removes two former prose-only interfaces from the
paper dependency graph.

\subsection{Exact formalization targets}

The most efficient formalization sequence is now the following.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Freeze one phase theorem returning the expansion in (2.2), the cap error,
and the adjacent-size inequalities with a common eventuality threshold.
\item Freeze one finite-dual theorem on $K_*$ returning uniform partition,
mean, variance, tilt, and optimizer convergence.
\item Export the root-corridor and normalized-slope theorem from those outputs;
do not restate the same asymptotics independently in Sections~4 and 5.
\item Formalize the Section~4 lower-tail adapter and the Section~5 finite-target
transport against those exact objects.
\item Formalize the four Section~7 declarations listed in Appendix~A and expose
their combined error as $\varepsilon_n^{\mathrm{pd}}$.
\item Assemble the already welded finite skeleton chain with the explicit
skeleton ledger $\Gamma_n^{\mathrm{skel}}$.
\item Formalize the conditioned residual estimate with output
$\Gamma_n^{\mathrm{att}}$, including the zero-residual branch.
\item Define $\Lambda_n$ by addition and replay the exact normalized
second-moment decomposition before invoking the welded amplifier.
\end{enumerate}

This organization prevents an asymptotic estimate from being proved twice
under different names and makes the quantifier order visible at every
interface.

\subsection{Unchanged publication gate}

The presence of complete candidate paper interfaces narrows the discovery
problem; it does not remove the verification problem. The publication switch
must remain false until all of the following occur on one integrated commit:

\begin{itemize}
\item independent line-by-line mathematical review of the new theorem-facing
sources;
\item exact Lean statements with the same constants, domains, and uniformity;
\item warning-fatal replay and axiom audit of the curated paper root;
\item synchronization of the manuscript ledger with the replayed declarations;
\item a final check that the high-skeleton and residual-attachment factors are
neither omitted nor charged twice.
\end{itemize}

Accordingly, the status of the main theorem remains fail-closed.

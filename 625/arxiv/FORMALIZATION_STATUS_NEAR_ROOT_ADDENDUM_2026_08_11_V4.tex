\section{Near-root strengthening addendum}
\label{app:near-root-strengthening-v4}

This addendum records the theorem-facing change introduced after the Version~3
status maps were assembled. It supersedes every earlier statement that the
signed profile must be placed at the midpoint of the ordinary and signed
first-moment roots. It does not promote the main theorem or any new declaration
to welded status.

\subsection{Candidate replacement interface}

The strengthened profile is defined by
\[
  k_{\mathrm{co}}
  =\left\lceil r_4^{\mathrm{co}}\right\rceil+1.
\]
The exact output required from the replacement Section~5 theorem is:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the displacement bound
$1\le k_{\mathrm{co}}-r_4^{\mathrm{co}}<2$;
\item inclusion of the complete interval
$[r_4^{\mathrm{co}},k_{\mathrm{co}}]$ in the uniform slope corridor;
\item phase-independent constants $0<c_{\mathrm{nr}}<C_{\mathrm{nr}}$ with
\[
  c_{\mathrm{nr}}(\log n)^2
  \le
  \log\mathbb E Z_{\mathbf k}^{\mathrm{sgn}}
  \le
  C_{\mathrm{nr}}(\log n)^2;
\]
\item an exact positive integer four-size profile satisfying both conservation
laws and having all coordinates of order $n/\log n$;
\item the full location asymptotic
\[
  k_\chi^- - k_{\mathrm{co}}
  =
  \left[
    \frac{(\log 2)^2}{4}A_4(\delta_n)+o(1)
  \right]
  \frac{n}{(\log n)^3}.
\]
\end{enumerate}

The replacement full-corner theorem consumes only the lower first-moment bound
and returns
\[
  \sum_{v(h)\le n/32}D(k-h)
  \le
  (k_{\mathrm{co}}+1)^4
  e^{-c_{\mathrm{nr}}(\log n)^2}
  =o(1).
\]
All other Section~7, Section~8, and Section~9 interfaces are unchanged.

\subsection{Dependency reduction}

The previous midpoint route used a displacement of order
$n/(\log n)^3$ to manufacture a first-moment exponent of order
$n/\log n$. The proof itself does not require that scale. Its two actual
consumers are:
\[
  \frac{1}{k_{\mathrm{co}}}
  \log\mathbb E Z_{\mathbf k}^{\mathrm{sgn}}=o(1)
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  (k_{\mathrm{co}}+1)^4
  \bigl(\mathbb E Z_{\mathbf k}^{\mathrm{sgn}}\bigr)^{-1}=o(1).
\]
A one-part displacement gives a $(\log n)^2$ exponent and proves both. The
formal dependency graph therefore no longer needs a midpoint-selection theorem
or a theorem transporting half of the root gap.

The strengthened closure chain is
\[
\begin{gathered}
  \text{uniform slope corridor}
  \longrightarrow
  \text{one-part objective margin},\\
  \text{finite optimizer positivity}
  \longrightarrow
  \text{exact tangent-rounded profile},\\
  \text{one-part margin}
  \longrightarrow
  \text{near-root full corner},\\
  \text{full root separation}
  +\text{sharpened seed exponent}
  \longrightarrow
  \text{doubled final coefficient}.
\end{gathered}
\]
The arrows denote theorem dependency, not equality.

\subsection{Recommended exact Lean sequence}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove the elementary ceiling lemma
$1\le\lceil x\rceil+1-x<2$.
\item Prove that a bounded class-count displacement changes $n/k$ by
$O((\log n)^2/n)$ throughout the root corridor.
\item Integrate the existing normalized slope estimate over a displacement in
$[1,2)$ to obtain two-sided $(\log n)^2$ bounds for the continuous signed
objective.
\item Reuse the finite optimizer and tangent-rounding declarations at
$k_{\mathrm{co}}=\lceil r_4^{\mathrm{co}}\rceil+1$.
\item Combine the objective estimate, tangent loss, and Stirling remainder into
the exact integer-profile first-moment theorem.
\item Replace the full-corner denominator theorem by the
$e^{-c(\log n)^2}$ estimate and replay the disjoint three-range assembly.
\item Consume the existing fused-kernel and critical-quarter attachment theorem
to obtain
$\Lambda_n\le C\sqrt n(\log n)^{3/2}$.
\item Formalize the amplification monomial calculation
$\sqrt{n\Lambda_n}/\log n
 =O(n^{3/4}(\log n)^{-1/4})$ and the full coefficient adapter.
\end{enumerate}

is a candidate theorem-facing strengthening. It must not be advertised as an
independently certified theorem until the following have all occurred on one
integrated commit:
\begin{itemize}

\item exact Lean replay of the bounded-displacement slope integration and the
revised full corner;
\item replay of the fused skeleton and residual attachment packages against the
same exact integer profile;
\item warning-fatal build, axiom audit, and final theorem-root replay;
\item synchronization of the manuscript coefficient with the final replayed
declaration.
\end{itemize}
Accordingly, the publication switch remains false.

The main theorem concerns G_n ~ G(n,1/2) through every integer n. It proves
a high-probability chromatic–cochromatic gap at least

  c n/(log n)^3,
  c = ((log 2)^2/4) log(200/153)
    = 0.03217587169793604... .

The stronger phase-resolved lower bound has leading coefficient
((log 2)^2/4) A_4(delta_n), with a deterministic error epsilon_n -> 0.
The phase constants are uniform, including sequences approaching integer
cutoffs. The new remark makes the rate explicit:

  epsilon_n = O(log log n / log n),

with a phase-independent implied constant. Equivalently, the remainder in
the unnormalized gap is O(n log log n/(log n)^4).

Why the refinement is justified
-------------------------------
Write L = log n and ell = log log n. The explicit overlap ledger in Section 9
already gives Lambda_n = O(n^(3/4) L). The amplification scales in (10.7)
therefore satisfy

  b_n = O(n^(7/8) L^(-1/2)),
  a_n = O(n^(15/16) L^(-7/4)) = o(n ell/L^4).

The root comparison (5.11) has error O(n ell/L^4), while the chromatic lower
location (4.1), rounded cocoloring location (5.13), and amplification cost
are smaller on this scale. Thus the normalized root error theta_n is
O(ell/L), and the normalized remaining cost eta_n is o(ell/L).
The already-defined epsilon_n = |theta_n| + eta_n has the asserted rate.

This result is stated and proved after the main theorem proof, with an
introductory pointer. It does not change the fixed uniform coefficient,
provide a numerical finite-n threshold, establish a matching upper bound,
or add a newly formalized Lean theorem. It exposes information already
contained in the written proof's explicit estimates.

CONTINUED MATHEMATICAL REVIEW
============================
The continued bounded review yields an explicit failure-probability bound
from estimates already proved in the manuscript. This is a further
quantitative consequence, not a repair to the previous theorem.

Put q=log 2. There are phase-independent constants C_1,C_2>0 and an integer N
such that every integer n>=N satisfies

 P{chi(G_n)-zeta(G_n)
      < [q^2 A_4(delta_n)/4-C_1 loglog n/log n] n/(log n)^3}
   <= C_2 (log n)^(2/q+1/2)/n.

The fixed-coefficient lower bound in the main theorem has the same
failure-probability bound after increasing N. Its positive uniform phase
margin absorbs C_1 loglog n/log n.

This is now Corollary 11.2. An introductory pointer identifies it, and
Appendix H states explicitly that the remainder and probability refinements
are proved in the manuscript and are not claimed as released Lean results.
The formalized leading coefficient is unchanged. No Lean run was performed.

Why the probability rate follows
--------------------------------
Write L=log n, ell=loglog n, beta=2/q+1/2. Appendix C permits every
deterministic r(n)>0 with a uniform constant. Choosing r=2L gives a
concentration failure at most n^-2. The simultaneous coloring event used
there follows from the density event in Section 10, whose failure is at
most exp(-c sqrt n). The extra class allowance is

 C[(sqrt(n Lambda_n)+sqrt(2nL))/L+n^(1/3)+1]
   =O(n^(7/8)L^(-1/2))=o(n ell/L^4),

because Lambda_n=O(n^(3/4)L). It fits inside the deterministic root error
already quantified in Remark 11.1.

The chromatic lower event has two separate failure terms. Rewriting (2.3)
gives the exact expansion

 log mu_(alpha+2)
   =-L+(2/q+1/2)ell+(delta_n-1)(L-ell)+O(1).

The phase-dependent term is nonpositive, including as delta_n approaches
one. The O(1) remainder is uniform, so the cap failure is at most
C_cap L^beta/n. Equation (4.5) adds exp(-c_chi L^3).

Thus the union bound contains all four contributions:

 C_cap L^beta/n + exp(-c_chi L^3) + n^-2 + exp(-c sqrt n).

No independence between these events is needed. A separate clique-cap
event is unnecessary: the chromatic lower event uses independent classes,
and the cocoloring construction has its class sizes fixed deterministically.
The last three terms are smaller than L^beta/n eventually. All estimates
and thresholds are independent of the phase.

The statement supplies existential constants and an eventual threshold;
it does not give numerical values for them. It does not claim a matching
upper bound, an optimal coefficient, or almost-sure convergence along all n.

separate final check confirmed that the inserted statement, proof, pointers
and formalization scope agree. Their detailed records accompany this report.
